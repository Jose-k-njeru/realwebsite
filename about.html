This is my life story

Birth and School life

I was born in Kiogogo village,Embu county in a family of 9 ,that is, 7 kids and parents where I grew up and joined St. Andrew's Ngoce primary school at the age of 4yrs.
After I did my class eight National Examination, I got 264 marks in my KCPE which is an average mark being ranked first in that rural school.

Later on I joined St. Albert's the Great Siakago Boy's High school an extra-county school there at Embu.
The learning was nice even if fees also was a problem but God helped me I was able to Complete my Studies.
At the end of my four years, I did my KCSE at the year 2023 and excelled with flying colours, despite the challenges I had, I got a B+.

Later on I joined University of Nairobi where I was admitted to Pursue Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry

Challenges in school

Challenges started in high school where the family friends who helped my parents to pay school fees stopped helping.
Ever since, life was not easy at all. Mom and Dad worked tirelessly in search of food and fees for me and my siblings.

I got excluded out of the classes several times with other students because we had not paid the remedial fee.
However, my parents never gave up they searched for money and since our family protocol is to put God first, God really intervene and answered our prayers.
My lovely Parents were able to complete the fees areas but not for remedial.
Even to-date I have never taken my KCSE certificate despite being released together with my leaving certificate.

Challenges never stopped there. Joining the University was another big problem. Still we never had enough money for all the requirements which were needed on the admission day.
My parents called for Harambee and gathered family and friends together for the raising of the money which was needed.

The event was successful and that how I got a chance to Join my dream University one of the best in Kenya.

But even here still I have some mere challenges such as food but that one I try my level best to curb them such as not going to the field to conserve the energy.
